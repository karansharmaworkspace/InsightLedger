# Research: Phase 12 — Demo Preparation & Documentation

## Demo Script Design

### Demo Flow
1. **Introduction**: Problem statement and solution overview
2. **Document ingestion**: Show PDF upload and processing
3. **Knowledge graph**: Visualize equipment relationships
4. **Chat interface**: Ask maintenance questions
5. **Maintenance intelligence**: Show predictive recommendations
6. **Compliance check**: Demonstrate gap analysis
7. **Lessons learned**: Show pattern detection
8. **Mobile demo**: Show field technician experience

### Sample Queries
- "What's the maintenance history for PUMP-A101?"
- "What are the compliance gaps for OISD-116?"
- "Show me equipment with predicted failures in next 30 days"
- "What patterns do you see in recent incidents?"

### Demo Data
- **Equipment**: 20+ industrial equipment types
- **Documents**: 50+ PDFs, spreadsheets, emails
- **Maintenance records**: 100+ work orders
- **Incident reports**: 50+ incidents
- **Compliance requirements**: 30+ regulatory items
