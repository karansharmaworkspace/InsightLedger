## Technical Design

### Demo Flow (5 minutes total)
```
1. Intro (0:00 - 0:30)
   - Problem: Industrial knowledge fragmentation
   - Solution: AI-powered knowledge intelligence

2. Document Ingestion (0:30 - 1:30)
   - Upload PDF maintenance report
   - Show OKF extraction
   - Show knowledge graph update

3. Knowledge Graph (1:30 - 2:30)
   - Navigate equipment relationships
   - Show compliance mapping
   - Highlight connected documents

4. Chat Interface (2:30 - 3:30)
   - Query: "What's the maintenance history for PUMP-A101?"
   - Query: "What are the compliance gaps for OISD-116?"
   - Show source citations and confidence scores

5. Maintenance Intelligence (3:30 - 4:15)
   - Show predictive maintenance recommendations
   - Show RCA for sample incident
   - Show equipment health dashboard

6. Compliance & Lessons (4:15 - 4:45)
   - Show compliance status traffic lights
   - Show detected patterns
   - Show proactive warnings

7. Conclusion (4:45 - 5:00)
   - Summary of impact
   - Future roadmap
```

### Sample Queries for Demo
1. "What's the maintenance history for PUMP-A101?"
2. "Show me equipment with predicted failures in next 30 days"
3. "What are the compliance gaps for OISD-116?"
4. "What patterns do you see in recent incidents?"
5. "Generate an RCA for the compressor failure on 2026-06-15"
